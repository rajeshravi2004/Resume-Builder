# Resume Studio brand

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The selected symbol combines an editable document with an AI sparkle, without letters or initials.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a premium, memorable SYMBOL-ONLY logo for Resume Studio, an AI-powered customizable resume and cover letter builder. Design a bespoke abstract PAPER-TO-SPARK emblem: a bold ivory ribbon curves and folds into an open document silhouette, then its upper-right corner transforms into a large brilliant four-point orange AI sparkle. The single connected visual idea is 'AI turns your experience into a beautiful document'. The sparkle is the unmistakable focal accent, integrated into the document's top-right contour with deliberate negative space. At the lower-left, two broad offset orange bars suggest editable content blocks. Not a conventional file icon; use graceful sculptural geometry and a smart negative-space cut, like a professionally crafted modern creative software brand. No letters, no initials, no words, no brain, no robot, no circuit board, no magic wand. Center one coherent emblem occupying 70% of the square. Solid deep navy #131c32 background across the entire square (opaque, not transparent); warm ivory #fffaf0 main mark and vivid burnt orange #e96939 sparkle. Flat vector-like rendering, perfectly clean edges, geometric optical balance, thick forms readable at favicon sizes. No mockup, no multiple designs, no shadows, no textures, no 3D. One final square logo.
